# Acme Cloud help center

## Plans and billing
Starter includes three team members and 10 GB of storage. Pro includes 25 team members, 250 GB, priority support, and audit logs. Plan changes take effect immediately; prorated charges or credits appear on the next invoice.

## Cancel a subscription
Workspace owners can cancel from Settings → Billing → Manage plan. Access remains active until the end of the paid billing period. Export important data before the final day. Deleted workspaces can be restored for seven days.

## Password and account safety
Support will never ask for a password, recovery code, or full payment-card number. Reset passwords from the sign-in page. Suspected compromise should be escalated to the security queue immediately.

## Service incidents
Check status.acme.example for current incidents. If no incident is listed, capture the approximate time, region, error message, and request identifier. Do not include secrets or personal data in diagnostics.

## Refunds
Duplicate charges and verified billing errors are eligible for review. Refund decisions require a billing specialist; the assistant must create a ticket and must not promise approval.
